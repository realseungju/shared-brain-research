# Claims — project-slug

## C001

- statement: the claim to be reviewed
- status: candidate
- evidence: TBD
- evidence_kind: unverified
- human_reviewed: no
- reason:

Allowed statuses: `candidate | supported | rejected`.

`supported` needs a real experiment path, `measured` or `estimated`, and
`human_reviewed: yes`. A result existing is not, by itself, support.
